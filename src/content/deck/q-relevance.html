How do we stay relevant, so people come back every week?

What we think today

  • The pantry and shopping list give people a reason to return, not just browse.
  • A weekly rhythm could work: check what you have, pick meals, shop.
  • Fresh recipes on a schedule, chosen for beginners.

What we don’t know yet

  • What actually brings someone back: reminders, seasons, savings?
  • How do we avoid being an app people try once and forget?
  • Which number to watch: meals cooked per week, rather than downloads?

Open question 4 of 7

Getting someone in once is marketing. Getting them back every week is the product. We need both to work together.
