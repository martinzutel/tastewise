Is the fridge and pantry scan a must-have, or a nice demo?

What we think today

  • Point the camera at the fridge or shelves and the pantry fills itself. No typing.
  • It’s exciting, and it shows in seconds what the app does.
  • Photos miss things in drawers and behind jars. Accuracy is the risk; the cost per scan is small.

What we don’t know yet

  • Would people value it enough to use it every week, or just try it once?
  • Should we test interest first, with a “coming soon” button?
  • Is it the hook that brings people in, even if they rarely use it?

Open question 6 of 7

We’d rather validate demand cheaply than build an impressive feature nobody uses twice.
