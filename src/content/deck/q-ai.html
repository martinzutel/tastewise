Is scanning your fridge a must-have, or a nice demo?

What we think today

  • It’s exciting, and great for showing what the app does.
  • But photos miss things, and adding a pantry by hand already takes about 30 seconds.
  • Running it is affordable. Accuracy is the real risk.

What we don’t know yet

  • Would people use it every week, or once?
  • Should we test interest first, with a “coming soon” button?
  • Is it the hook that gets people in, even if they rarely use it?

Open question 6 of 7

We’d rather validate demand cheaply than build an impressive feature nobody uses twice.
