Why would someone use Tastewise instead of asking a chatbot?

What we think today

  • A chat answer is gone tomorrow. Tastewise remembers your pantry, list and favorites.
  • Tested recipes with exact amounts, not invented ones.
  • Built for the moment of cooking: one hand, one step at a time.

What we don’t know yet

  • Do people care about these differences enough to download an app?
  • How do we say it without sounding defensive?
  • Should AI live inside Tastewise rather than be something we compete with?
