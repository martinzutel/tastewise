Beginner cooks don’t lack recipes.
They lack a starting point.

“What do I make tonight?”

Endless recipe sites, but no idea which ones fit what’s actually in the kitchen.

“I bought it twice”

Shopping without knowing what you have means duplicates, waste and missing one key item.

“What if I mess it up?”

Long recipe pages are hard to follow while cooking, so people give up and order in.

Who we’re building for: students and young adults learning to cook, mostly on their phones.

These three moments come from our own experience and early conversations, not from formal research yet. That’s one of the things we’d like to validate.
