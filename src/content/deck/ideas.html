The three ideas at the heart of Tastewise

Built

Your pantry

Tell Tastewise what’s in your kitchen once. From then on, every recipe is matched against what you actually have.

10 ingredients in, 28 recipes you can start on.

Built

Missing items

Each recipe shows exactly what you’re missing. One tap puts it on a single shopping list, combined and sorted by aisle.

“Shakshuka: 80% ready. Missing paprika and cilantro.”

Next idea, not built

Fridge and pantry scan

Point your camera at the fridge or the shelves and AI fills in your pantry for you. No typing at all.

The idea that makes the first two effortless.

The first two are real and working today. The scan is the idea we’re most excited about, and the one we most want to validate before building.
