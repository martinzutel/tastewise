Where we want to go

For students and young adults learning to cook, mostly on their phones.

Truly useful

Solve a real weekly problem, not add another recipe catalogue.

High quality

Fewer, better recipes that actually work for beginners.

Honest

No fake reviews or inflated claims. Clear about what’s estimated.

Smarter over time
