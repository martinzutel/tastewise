Who pays, and for what?

What we think today

  • The core stays free, so anyone can start.
  • A small “Plus” plan for extras like the fridge scan or an assistant.
  • Later, grocery partnerships: send your list to a store.
  • AI costs per use are small. Ads would hurt trust.

What we don’t know yet

  • Will beginners, often students, value it enough to pay anything?
  • Which feature is actually worth paying for?
  • When should we start charging?

Open question 5 of 7

We looked at AI costs: running a fridge scan on a small model costs a fraction of a cent. So the real question isn’t how to afford AI, it’s whether people will pay for value.
