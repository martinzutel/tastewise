Do we do one thing brilliantly, or everything well?

What we think today

  • Recipe sites already offer millions of free recipes. We can’t win on size.
  • Our sharpest idea is cooking from your pantry: see what you can make with what you have, and what’s missing.
  • Start there, then grow into planning and AI.

What we don’t know yet

  • Is “I don’t know what to cook with what I have” a big enough everyday problem that people would change how they find recipes?
  • Which one sentence makes someone want to try it?
  • Is “for beginner cooks” the right angle, or too narrow?

Open question 1 of 7

Our instinct says focus on the pantry. The open part is whether that problem hurts enough, often enough, for people to adopt a new app for it.
